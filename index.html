Grow and evolve your swarm

Infect humans, choose mutations and shape your evolution tree. Cluster and spread to break through the defenders in this action survival game.

Controls and rewards

Play in landscape. Drag one finger to lead the swarm, and use the formation and ability buttons. After a run, optionally watch an ad to receive the same amount of genes again, once per result. Base rewards and the next run remain available without ads.

Local saves

Progress and settings are stored on your device. Deleting the app can remove them, subject to device backups. No login or in-app purchases are required.
